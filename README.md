## 🏥 Overview
**AEGIS CARE** is a state-of-the-art intelligent hospital infrastructure platform designed to modernize acute healthcare delivery through:
- Real-time acute bed & ward orchestration (ICU, Trauma ER, Surgical PACU, Isolation Units).
- Dynamic patient flow & emergency triage (ESI-based automatic queue prioritization).
- IoT medical telemetry & environmental mesh monitoring (Medical liquid oxygen pressure, ultra-cold freezer banks, negative-pressure isolation airflow, RTLS crash carts).
- AI clinical decision support & automated early warning (NEWS2 & Sepsis-3 early intervention protocols).
- Sub-millisecond HL7 / FHIR R4 interoperability and cryptographic zero-trust compliance.

## 🚀 Features & Interactive Capabilities

1. **Interactive Background Neural Constellation:**
   - HTML5 Canvas particle mesh with proximity physics and mouse reactivity simulating hospital telemetry packet transit.
2. **Real-time Live ECG Oscilloscope:**
   - Canvas-rendered continuous cardiac P-Q-R-S-T waveform with live BPM calculations.
3. **Interactive Hospital Command Center:**
   - **Bed & Ward Matrix:** 12 interactive beds with real clinical vitals. Click any bed to view vitals, order CPOE medications, or authorize bed turnover.
   - **Mass Casualty / Code Orange Influx Simulator:** Trigger incoming helicopter trauma arrivals to watch the AI automatically reserve surgical suites, dispatch trauma surgeon alerts, and stage blood bank units.
   - **IoT Telemetry Sandbox:** Test emergency oxygen pressure failovers with cryogenic manifold auto-switching.
   - **Predictive AI Sepsis-3 Engine:** Inspect real-time deteriorating patient metrics and execute the 1-hour surviving sepsis bundle.
4. **Perspective Lens (Role Switcher):**
   - Toggle viewpoints for Hospital Operations Director, Chief Medical Officer, Chief Nursing Officer, or Healthcare IT Architect.
5. **Hospital Impact & Capacity ROI Calculator:**
   - Live sliders for Bed Capacity and Daily Emergency Census calculating annual staff hours returned and financial dividend.
6. **Built-in Web Audio Telemetry Synthesizer:**
   - Zero-dependency browser audio engine providing clinical beeps, heartbeat audio, and critical trauma alarms (with mute toggle).
